Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operations. Messages are kept as long as needed to answer them, or as the law requires. Then they are deleted.

The site is served only over https. No system is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee that data will never be lost or disclosed.

You may ask to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it or to delete it, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.
