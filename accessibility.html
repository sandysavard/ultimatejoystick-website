Accessibility Statement

Vantage Point Software Solutions wants Ultimate Joystick to be usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology.

Effective date: 2026-11-10

1. Our commitment

We aim for the site to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. The site is built with semantic HTML, a skip link to the main content, visible keyboard focus, text alternatives for images, labelled forms and one main heading per page.

2. No overlays

We do not use accessibility overlays or widgets. Accessibility is built into the pages themselves, so it works with the assistive technology you already use.

3. Known limits

Some content from other services (for example embedded videos) is outside our control and may not fully meet the same standard. We review the site when it changes and fix the barriers we find.

4. Report a barrier

If something on the site is hard or impossible for you to use, tell us at support@ultimatejoystick.com. Please include the page address and what went wrong. We will reply and work on a fix, and we can provide the information in another format.
